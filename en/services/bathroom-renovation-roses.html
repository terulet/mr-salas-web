Bathroom renovation in Roses

Replace a bath with a shower or remodel the whole bathroom. Manolo reviews the drainage, layout and finishes with you before agreeing the work.

Ask on WhatsApp

What we check at the first visit

  • We check the available floor depth and drainage to work out how to fit the shower tray and finish the entrance.
  • We look at waterproofing and the junctions between the floor, walls and screen, especially around the shower.
  • For a full refit, we agree where the basin, toilet and storage will go before choosing the tiles.
Will I need to replace all the tiles when I remove the bath?

Sometimes only the area around the bath needs to be retiled. We look at the condition of the existing tiles and how the new finish will meet them.

We work from Roses. If your property is in another town in Girona province, tell us where it is so we can confirm whether we can travel there.
