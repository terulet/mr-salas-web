Home renovations in Roses, Spain

Renovating a house, flat or holiday home in Roses? Walk through the property with Manolo Rodríguez to decide what to keep, repair or change.

Ask on WhatsApp

What we check at the first visit

  • We look at how you use each room before discussing changes to the layout, storage and finishes.
  • We check which existing services can stay and which need attention before walls and floors are closed up.
  • If you use the property seasonally, we discuss access dates and which parts of the work make sense to do together.
Can I renovate my flat in stages?

We discuss this at the first visit. Some rooms can be handled separately. If work in different rooms shares plumbing, wiring or flooring, we consider those connections before agreeing the order.

We work from Roses. If your property is in another town in Girona province, tell us where it is so we can confirm whether we can travel there.
