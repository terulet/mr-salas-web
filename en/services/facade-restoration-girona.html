Façade restoration in Girona province

From Roses, M.R. Salas assesses façades with damaged render, cracks or signs of damp. Tell us where the property is and what you have noticed.

What we check at the first visit

  • We check where render is coming away and examine the surface around windows and balconies.
  • We look at potential paths for water and at exposed junctions before deciding between a local repair and wider work.
  • Height, access and the condition of the surface affect how the job can be carried out and how it should be priced.
Can I paint straight over damp patches on a façade?

First identify the source of the damp and check the condition of the underlying surface. Those findings determine which repair and finish are suitable.

We work from Roses. If your property is in another town in Girona province, tell us where it is so we can confirm whether we can travel there.
