Kitchen renovation in Roses

Plan a kitchen that works for you every day. At the property, Manolo reviews the layout, finishes and which existing features you could keep.

What we check at the first visit

  • We look at the route between the worktop, sink and hob and leave enough room for cupboards and appliances to open.
  • Before moving the sink or opening the kitchen to the living area, we check the plumbing, drainage, ventilation and wall.
  • We agree the order for wall finishes, worktops and cabinetry so the junctions are ready before fitting.
Can I open my kitchen into the living room?

It depends on the wall and the services that run through it. A site visit helps identify the checks needed before changing the layout.

We work from Roses. If your property is in another town in Girona province, tell us where it is so we can confirm whether we can travel there.
